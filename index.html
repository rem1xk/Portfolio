From brief
to final frame.

A clear starting point makes better work. Here’s how we can shape the project together.

  1. Share the brief

    Tell me the audience, deliverables, deadline and budget range. References and existing assets help.

  2. Agree the direction

    We define scope, visual approach and review stages before production starts.

  3. Build & refine

    Animation and editing take shape, with feedback focused on the agreed direction.

  4. Finish for the format

    We check the final film and prepare the deliverables agreed in the brief.

Before we start.

Can you work with an existing idea or brand style?

Yes. Share your concept, brand guidelines and existing assets with the brief so we can define my part in the production.

What does a project cost?

It depends on scope, complexity, formats and timing. Send your brief and budget range, and we can discuss a quote for your project.

What should I send first?

A short description, the intended audience, reference links, deliverables and your target deadline. An early outline is enough to start the conversation.
